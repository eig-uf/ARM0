# ARM0

> ARM0 is the Embodied Intelligence Group's initiative to design and
build a six-degree-of-freedom robotic arm at the University of Florida.
Through ARM0, we aim to give members practical experience across mechanical
design, electronics, and software while creating a documented platform for
future club projects.
